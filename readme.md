# Yo, it's lowkey

A thoughtfulness system for relationships. Track the people who matter, get personalized nudges to reach out, and show up meaningfully.

## Features

- ✅ **In-app notification display** - Notifications show as banners when the app is open
- ✅ **On-demand nudge generator** - Long-press any person → Generate personalized nudge
- ✅ **Smart notification content** - Personalized nudges based on relationship type, love language, interests, and context
- 📱 **People management** - Add, edit, and organize the people in your life
- 🔔 **Smart scheduling** - Notifications adapt to nudge frequency and relationship type
- 🎯 **Contextual nudges** - Suggestions tailored to each person's interests and your relationship
